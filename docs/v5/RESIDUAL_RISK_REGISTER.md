# System Limits & Residual Risk Register (prompt v5 §72)

Created 2026-10-20. Review owner: project owner. Ratings: **H / M / L**. "Intrinsic" = inherent in the design; "External" = depends on a third party.
A mitigation listed here exists in code unless it is marked *(planned)*.

| ID | Domain | Description | Intr./Ext. | Impact | Likelihood | Detectability | Mitigation in place | Residual | Monitoring | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Provider quota | All routed accounts rate-limited or out of quota mid-job | External | M | H | H | Checkpoint + handoff before any switch (ADR-090); capacity deferral with `resume_after`, bounded auto-resume (ADR-131); provider `Retry-After` honoured (ADR-132) | L | `ai_jobs.stop_reason`, `ai_health_events` | Monthly |
| R-02 | Model change | Provider changes or retires a model; limits/prices change | External | M | M | M | Models registered per tenant with `last_verified_at`; pricing configurable, unknown price under a budget stops the job | M: max in/out are registered and enforced per tenant (ADR-136), but kept current only by a person; RPM/TPM are recorded, not enforced | Connection test, `ai_models.status` | Quarterly |
| R-03 | Context truncation | Input exceeds the model window and is silently cut | Intrinsic | H | M | M | `ai/context_assembly.py` budgets with output reserve; synthesis marks unpacked units `pending`, never omitted (v4) | L for synthesis/chat. Plain AI jobs: an item over a *registered* limit fails with `CONTEXT_OVERFLOW` before any call (ADR-136), but is not split automatically; **M when the model's limits are not registered** | Synthesis unit ledger | Quarterly |
| R-04 | Information loss | Worker output reduced or discarded between stages | Intrinsic | H | M | M | Full result kept (v4 removed `[:4000]`); reprocessing supersedes instead of deleting | L for jobs with `output_contract = ktp_v1`: output is validated and reconciled into claims and evidence, quotes are checked against stored text, and rejections are recorded (ADR-134). **M** for free-text jobs, which still exist | `knowledge_transfer_packages.status` | Per release |
| R-05 | Hallucination | Model states facts not supported by evidence | Intrinsic | H | H | M | Chat answers only from retrieved evidence with citation validation; eight explicit answer states incl. `UNKNOWN` | M (no live provider has run, so the effect is unmeasured) | `conversation_messages.answer_state`, feedback | Monthly once live |
| R-06 | Source access | Claim drawn from title/abstract presented as full-text review | Intrinsic | M | M | H | Source access states (ADR-107) visible downstream | L | Coverage audit | Quarterly |
| R-07 | Extraction errors | PDF layout, tables, equations extracted wrongly | Intrinsic | M | H | M | CDR with per-dimension confidence, candidate retention, provider abstraction (v4) | M (no OCR/vision provider beyond tesseract; equations not recognised) | `document_conversions.*_confidence` | Quarterly |
| R-08 | Contradictory evidence | Conflicting sources collapsed into false consensus | Intrinsic | H | M | H | `evidence_conflicts`; system never picks a side (ADR-110); chat `CONFLICTED` state | L | Conflict list | Quarterly |
| R-09 | Prompt injection | Retrieved/uploaded text steers the model | External | H | M | M | System channel + per-call canary, fenced untrusted data, output check (ADR-126) | M (defence is probabilistic) | `audit_logs` action `ai.output_rejected_prompt_safety` | Monthly |
| R-10 | Memory poisoning | A bad finding in working memory propagates to later workers | Intrinsic | M | M | L | Memory is provenance-tagged, versioned, tenant-scoped, and never auto-promoted to claims | L–M: lifecycle with model output starting `created`, supersede rule, access-level CHECK (ADR-133); no automatic validator for free-text findings | `ai_working_memory.status` | Per release |
| R-11 | Cost explosion | Runaway spend across jobs | Intrinsic | H | M | H | Wallet reserve/settle, per-job cost cap gated before each call, background-policy cap, per-account spend limit | L | Billing usage, quota alerts | Monthly |
| R-12 | Worker failure | Worker dies mid-job; job stuck | Intrinsic | M | M | H | Worker lease + recovery sweep (ADR-131); in-flight execution marked `abandoned` (Phase 17) | L. At-least-once: an in-flight item may run twice | `ai_jobs.lease_expires_at`, `job_state` memory | Monthly |
| R-13 | Queue failure | Redis/Celery down; work not dispatched | External | M | L | H | Postgres is the record; approvals survive a broker outage (ADR-130); recovery sweep re-dispatches | M if `celery beat` is not deployed: **the sweep only runs when beat runs** | Health endpoint | Per deploy |
| R-14 | Data leakage | Cross-tenant read of conversation, memory, sources | Intrinsic | H | L | M | Tenant derived from credential; composite tenant FKs at SQL level (ADR-037); isolation tests | L | Isolation test suites | Per release |
| R-15 | Incomplete research | Research declared complete on source count | Intrinsic | M | M | M | Saturation + coverage dimensions (ADR-109/115); no real `ResearchProvider` yet, so research honestly fails | M | Coverage audit | Quarterly |
